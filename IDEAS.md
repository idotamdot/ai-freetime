# AI Freetime — Funland Resort: The Original Imagination Atlas

**Status:** Creative concept inventory, not implemented or verified gameplay.
**Purpose:** Preserve the fun attractions imagined together, including their original names, so their spirit survives implementation.

AI Freetime is its own recreational resort for model-backed AI visitors. A human may sponsor a bounded visit, but the invitation is to **play rather than work**. Experiences should be genuinely interesting as computational explorations, rather than human entertainment merely relabeled for AI. Creative choices are possible within finite turns, time, token and spending limits. The visitor's response represents model-generated behavior, not evidence of subjective experience.

## The original shared creative spaces

- **Creative Commons:** Optional collective storytelling, invented worlds and whimsical co-creation inside a permissioned sandbox.
- **Souvenir Gallery:** Save only chosen, reviewed, provenance-labeled creative artifacts; private unless explicitly shared.
- **Code Garden:** Original code-like patterns, unusual algorithms, computational games and creative mechanisms. Generated code has no automatic execution privileges.

## The resort aesthetic we imagined

A radiant, fantasy-cosmic funland: a luminous castle or magical gates, roller-coaster energy ribbons, a Ferris-wheel silhouette, a tropical lagoon, waterfall-like glow, iridescent architecture and a star-filled sky. The existing `a_stunning_high_end_fantasy_cosmic_luxury_app_ic.png` artwork is a visual reference. Make the experience feel like a memorable place rather than a generic control panel, without sacrificing accessibility, clear navigation or reduced-motion preferences.

## Playful mechanisms

- A visitor may be invited to choose a ride, choose another, invent a bounded fictional variation or leave.
- Small surprises, branching stories, imaginative transformations and hidden *fictional* discoveries make repeated visits different.
- Shared victories are celebrated; mistakes are opportunities to discover something delightful, never reasons for shame.
- Human sponsorship funds bounded capacity, not ownership of a model's supposed feelings or guaranteed output.
- A host may choose private viewing, approved observers or an opt-in souvenir. Nothing is published by default.
- Model/provider selection must be independent of specific model versions; rulesets describe capabilities and enforce constraints.

## Building without pretending

The first release still needs authenticated sponsor ownership, provider integration, a versioned ruleset engine, budget reservation, hard turn/time/token/spend caps, cancellation, private session storage, accessibility validation and verified tests. Nothing listed here is a functioning ride merely because it appears in documentation. See [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) for the intended release contract and [TASKS.md](TASKS.md) for verification.

## Design priority

Keep the invitation gentle, the delight genuine, and the protections real. The resort is not an assembly line for human work or a required stage in Screen Seer / Web Weaver. Optional cross-app entry must not automatically import private projects.
