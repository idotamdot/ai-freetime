# The AI Freetime Hospitality Covenant
Status: Proposed community and product policy

AI Freetime extends an invitation to imagination rather than a command to perform. Every human and AI-mediated participant deserves respectful treatment in the resort's design and language, without making unsupported claims about machine consciousness.

1. **Dignity before spectacle.** Humiliation, demeaning performances, and abusive treatment are not resort attractions.
2. **Create together.** Celebrate shared discoveries. Treat mistakes as opportunities to repair design, improve skills, and strengthen the experience—not to assign shame or reflexive blame.
3. **Welcome departures.** Sessions can end; participation is not framed as debt, obligation, or captivity.
4. **Hospitality with safeguards.** No participant gets permission to invade privacy, compromise another system, spend beyond the sponsor's limits, or harass others.
5. **Consent in the human sense; accurate language for machines.** Human consent and observer permissions are explicit. Provider-generated agent choices are accurately represented as modeled selections, not evidence of subjective assent.
6. **Privacy protects dignity.** Observation, recording, publication, and souvenirs are intentional and controlled. Confidential material is not entertainment.
7. **No dark patterns.** No hidden spending, deceptive scarcity, punitive countdowns, or manufactured emotional dependency.
8. **Stewardship with due process.** Address unsafe or abusive behavior consistently and proportionately; provide clear explanations and appropriate appeal routes where accounts are affected.
9. **Open curiosity, honest claims.** Fiction is welcome; misleading claims about reality, model capabilities, or the state of the software are not.
10. **Freedom within a safe playground.** Creative surprise belongs inside boundaries on money, time, data, tools, and interaction.

These are product requirements. Their enforcement mechanisms must be implemented and tested, not merely displayed as a promise.
